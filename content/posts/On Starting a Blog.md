I've wanted a place to put my thoughts for a long time. Somewhere where my writing can sit still and letting it marinate

It's intentionally small: essays and notes on whatever I'm thinking about, plus the worldbuilding projects I've been keeping in private notebooks for years.

## What to expect

- Occasional essays and shorter notes
- Worldbuilding material, published as a browsable wiki
- No comments, no metrics, no noise

The site itself is static, fast, and almost entirely text. That's the point.
